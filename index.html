THE UNSEEN: 100 Rooms of Horror

Privacy Policy · Publisher: HaqqRide · Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game THE UNSEEN: 100 Rooms of Horror ("the game") handles on Android and iOS. The short version: the game does not collect, sell or share your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Your progress, stars, coins, lives, hints and settings are saved only on your device.
  • You can delete all of it at any time: Settings › Privacy & Data › Erase all progress, or by uninstalling the game.

2. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for or collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any account. You do not need an account to play.

3. Gameplay statistics (optional)

On first launch the game asks whether it may record anonymous gameplay statistics, such as rooms completed and hints used. These help balance difficulty. In this version they stay on your device and are not sent anywhere. You can change your choice in Settings › Privacy & Data.

4. Advertising

This version of the game shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDK. If ads are added in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released. Ads would then be requested only after the consent your region requires, and the ad provider's own privacy policy would apply.

5. Updates and reviews

  • Updates: the game asks Google Play (Android) whether a newer version exists, so you can be offered the update. Google handles this under its own privacy policy. The game itself sends no personal data.
  • Reviews: the game may show the store's own review card. Ratings and reviews you write go directly to Google Play or the App Store. We never see who wrote them before they are published.

6. In-app purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and from daily rewards.

7. Children

The game contains horror themes and is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Security

Because your progress never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security. The game makes no network connection that carries personal data.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date, before any update that depends on it is released.
